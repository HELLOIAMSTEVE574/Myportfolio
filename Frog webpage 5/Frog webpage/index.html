Frogs🐸

Frogs are very intresting, they can go from harmless small creatures to some of the dealiest animals so venomous that one touch can be fatal.

Golden poison frog

The golden poison frog is the most dangerous frog, it's venom is so deadly that one singular frog can carry enough poison to take out 10-20 full grown adaults. A single touch from this frog can be fatal.

Glass frog

Glass frogs are small unique frogs. They have very thin skin which makes them transparent and can help them camouflag. Although they can camouflage, they are mostly defenseless when spotted by a predator.

Flying frog

Flying frogs can glide in the air using their large webbed feet. They live in trees and can glide from one tree to another without touching the floor helping them avoid predators.

Desert rain frogs

Desert rain frog sound

Desert rain frogs are small frogs that live in dry areas in southern Africa. They can survive a long time without water by hiding in the sand and staying cool. When threatened, instead of hoping away it inflates it's body and does a high pitch squeak to scare away other animals/insects.
